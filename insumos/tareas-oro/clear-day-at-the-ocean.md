## Enunciado original (EN, literal, p. 43)

> **A Clear Day at the Ocean**
> Perhaps you have observed at the ocean, that due the Earth's curvature, even with a clear view you only can see a certain distance. With the rule
> w² = 13 h
> you can estimate the range of sight in kilometers (w), depending on how high above the ground your eyes are (h). You have to use h in meters.
> • How far can you see, if your eyes are 1.60 m above the ground?
> • Make a table for the range of sight with h = 5 m (10 m; 20 m; 40 m; 80 m). How does the range of sight, w, change, if the height h is quadrupled?

(Incluye una figura con las variables w y h.)

## Notas de la autora útiles para puntuar (pp. 44–45)

- "A typical task you can find in several versions in many school books and has a real context."
- "Students do not have to make assumptions, because the real situation is already simplified by the given rule."
- "It is obvious that this task is not a modeling problem, even though we have a real context. Learners do not pass through all the phases of the modeling cycle; they are only exercising rules. Ultimately the context of this task is not necessary for the students."
