Texto original  
Students, for example, have to explain how they see the ageing of the population in the table. Moreover, they have to formulate what it means that the 
percentages no longer change in the long run and they have to explain this phenomenon using the first observation above.  
 
In the previous section calculations led to two observations that were formulated in terms of the long-term growth factor and the long term age distribution. In 
this section, the central question is: how can the long-term growth factor and the long-term age distribution be calculated in a more ‘mathematical’ way?
