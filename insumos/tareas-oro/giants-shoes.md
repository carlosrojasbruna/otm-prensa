Texto original  
In a sports centre on the Philippines, Florentino Anonuevo Jr. polishes a pair of shoes. They are, according to the Guinness Book of Records, the world’s biggest, 
with a width of 2.37 m and a length of 5.29 m. Approximately how tall would a giant be for these shoes to fit? Explain your solution.
