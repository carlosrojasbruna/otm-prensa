## Enunciado original (EN, literal, p. 106)

> In the bay of Massachusetts, a lighthouse called "Boston light" was built in 1716, measuring 31 m (102 feet) in height. Its beacon was meant to warn ships that they were approaching the coast.
> How far, approximately, was a ship when it saw the lighthouse for the first time? Explain your solution.

## Notas de la autora útiles para puntuar (pp. 106–109)

- "This problem is not trivial for school students, nor for pre- and in-service teachers."
- "This modeling task also fulfills the required criteria of a good modeling problem (see Sect. 3.1)."
- Los tres escenarios transcritos (Catherine/Luke, Kevin/Werner, Maike/Lars) muestran: modelo real con dibujo, modelo matemático con la Tierra como círculo y Pitágoras con el radio terrestre, error intra-matemático (x² + r² = (L + r)² mal resuelto), interpretación de unidades (18 km) y la necesidad de validar.
- Para el pipeline: estos escenarios son un ejemplo publicado del tipo de "escenarios simulados" que debe producir la guía docente (PLT02 §4).
